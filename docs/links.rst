Telescope-specific pages
========================

Some telescopes have dedicated Visplot pages with pre-configured settings. These are accessible at ``https://www.visplot.com/[telescope_code]``. Here is a list of available telescope-specific pages:

 
- Anglo-Australian Telescope (AAT): https://www.visplot.com/aat
- Calar Alto Observatory (CAHA): https://www.visplot.com/caha
- Clarence T. Jones Observatory (CTJO): https://www.visplot.com/ctjo
- Dark Sky Observatory (DSO): https://www.visplot.com/dso 
- Gran Telescopio de Canarias (GTC): https://www.visplot.com/gtc
- Harlam J. Smith Telescope (HJST): https://www.visplot.com/hjst
- Hobby-Eberly Telescope (HET): https://www.visplot.com/het 
- Isaac Newton Telescope (INT): https://www.visplot.com/int
- Kennon Observatory (CDK): https://www.visplot.com/cdk 
- Large Binocular Telescope (LBT): https://www.visplot.com/lbt 
- MMT Observatory (MMT): https://www.visplot.com/mmt 
- New Technology Telescope (NTT): https://www.visplot.com/ntt 
- Nordic Optical Telescope (NOT): https://www.visplot.com/not
- Observatorio Astrofísico de Javalambre (OAJ): https://www.visplot.com/oaj
- Otto Struve Telescope (OST): https://www.visplot.com/ost
- Southern African Large Telescope (SALT): https://www.visplot.com/salt
- Telescopio Nazionale Galileo (TNG): https://www.visplot.com/tng 
- Vera C. Rubin Observatory (LSST): https://www.visplot.com/lsst 
- Very Large Telescope (VLT): https://www.visplot.com/vlt
- William Herschel Telescope (WHT): https://www.visplot.com/wht
- Wyoming Infrared Observatory (WIRO): https://www.visplot.com/


(Note: This list may not be exhaustive; check the main Visplot site for the latest additions.)
